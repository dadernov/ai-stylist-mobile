Privacy Policy

AI Stylist app for iOS. Version of 28 September 2026. If versions differ, the Russian text prevails.

1. Who processes your data

The app and its server are run by an individual developer (the data controller). For any questions about your data, or requests to access, correct or delete it: mrbdigital@proton.me. We reply within 30 days.

3. Your face in photos

To keep the generator from distorting your face and background, the server locates the face with a local model and restores the original pixels of the head and background after generation. We don't create biometric templates, don't identify people and don't store facial data. Photo metadata (including EXIF location tags) is removed before photos are sent to the generation service.

When you choose a try-on photo, the server checks the frame with the same local models (whether your face, the top of your head and your feet are visible, whether an arm is raised, whether other people are in the frame) and suggests how to improve it. For this check the photo is not sent anywhere and is not stored.

4. Consent

Before photos are sent to AI processing for the first time, the app explains where they go and asks for your consent. Without it, AI analysis and try-on don't run; everything else works. You can withdraw consent in Profile → “Consent to AI photo processing”. Upload only your own photos or photos of people who agreed to it.

5. Who receives data

  • fal.ai (Features & Labels, Inc., USA) — try-on photos, only during generation. The image is created by ByteDance's Seedream model; the request may be processed on ByteDance (BytePlus) servers. Their terms: fal.ai/legal/privacy-policy, byteplus.com/en/legal/privacy-policy.
  • FASHN AI (fashn.ai) — fallback generation service, same photos and retention. Their terms: fashn.ai/privacy-policy.
  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — coordinates for the weather forecast, directly from the app.
  • The hosting provider of the app server (data centre in Austria, EU) — technical hosting.

Data leaves the user's country only for these recipients and purposes. The app contains no ads or analytics trackers, doesn't track you across apps and doesn't sell data.

6. Your rights and deletion

You can find out what data we hold about you, correct or delete it, and withdraw consent. Delete your account in the app: Profile → “Delete account”. The account, sessions, try-on results and counters are removed from the server immediately, together with all app data on the device. Service database backups are kept for 14 days and contain no images; a deleted account disappears from them when they expire.

7. Security

Connections are protected by HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as a strong hash. Generation is available only to signed-in users, and the number of requests is limited.

8. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13. If you learn that a child has given us data, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

A new version is published on this page with its date. If the way photos are shared changes, the app will ask for your consent again.
