How to delete your account

Profile → “Delete account” → confirm. Your account and data are deleted on the server and on the device. If you can't access the app, email us from the login you want deleted.

You need a full-body photo with one clearly visible face and at least one item with a photo. If you see a limit message, the daily allowance resets tomorrow.

How to save the result

Under the try-on result, tap “Save to gallery” and allow saving photos.
